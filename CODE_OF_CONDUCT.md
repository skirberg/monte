# Code of conduct

Monte is for everyone who wants to understand statistics, from their first average to multiple regression. Anyone taking part here, in issues, pull requests and discussions, can ask a basic question and get a kind answer.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short:

- **Do:** be kind, assume good faith, explain rather than correct, and accept feedback on your work gracefully.
- **Don't:** harass anyone, use sexual language or imagery, make personal or political attacks, or publish anyone's private information.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) option on the comment, issue or pull request. Reports reach the maintainer privately and are handled in confidence. The maintainer may edit, hide or remove contributions, and block anyone who breaks these rules, as the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe.
