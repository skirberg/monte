# Correlation: direction and strength

> Correlation r is covariance scaled by both SDs: r = Cov(X, Y) ÷ (σx · σy). It has no units and runs from −1 to 1. Near ±1, the points hug a straight line.

**Question:** Correlation runs from −1 to 1. Which one is the strongest link?

- **−0.9** (right answer): Yes. Strength is how far from 0. The sign only gives the direction.
- 0.5: 0.5 is moderate. −0.9 is much closer to a perfect line.
- 0: 0 means no straight-line link at all.

Correlation r is covariance scaled by both SDs: r = Cov(X, Y) ÷ (σx · σy). It has no units and runs from −1 to 1. Near ±1, the points hug a straight line.

**Question:** σx = 10, σy = 20, Cov = 100. What is r?

- 5: Divide by 10 × 20, not by 20.
- **0.5** (right answer): Yes. 100 ÷ (10 × 20) = 0.5.
- 100: That's the covariance. Divide by both SDs.

Correlation is covariance with the units taken out, so you can compare any pair of variables.
