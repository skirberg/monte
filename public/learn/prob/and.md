# Both happen: multiply

> Multiplication rule: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B given A). If A doesn't change the chances for B (independent), that's just P(A) × P(B). And means multiply.

**Question:** You flip a coin twice. What's the chance of two heads?

- 1: Adding 1/2 + 1/2 is the mistake. For both, multiply.
- **1/4** (right answer): Yes. Half the time the first is heads, and half of those the second is too.
- 1/2: That's one flip. Two heads needs both to go your way.

Multiplication rule: P(A and B) = P(A) × P(B given A). If A doesn't change the chances for B (independent), that's just P(A) × P(B).

**Question:** Draw 2 cards without putting the first back. P(both aces)?

- 4/52 + 4/51: For both to happen, multiply, don't add.
- **4/52 × 3/51** (right answer): Yes. After one ace is gone, 3 aces are left in 51 cards.
- 4/52 × 4/52: That puts the first card back. Without replacement, the second draw changes.

And means multiply. Or means add, then subtract the overlap.
