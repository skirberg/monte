# A long-run share

> A probability is a number from 0 (never) to 1 (certain): the share of times an event happens if you could repeat the situation many times.

**Question:** A weather app says 30% chance of rain tomorrow. What does that mean?

- It rains in 30% of the area: It's the chance for your spot, not a share of the map.
- **On days like this, it rains about 3 times in 10** (right answer): Yes. A probability is the share of times something happens over many repeats.
- It will rain for 30% of the day: It's about whether it rains, not for how long.

A probability is a number from 0 (never) to 1 (certain): the share of times an event happens if you could repeat the situation many times. The chances of all possible outcomes add to 1.

**Question:** The chance a flight is late is 0.2. What's the chance it's not late?

- You can't tell: You can: not late is everything else, 1 − 0.2.
- **0.8** (right answer): Yes. Late or not late covers everything, so they add to 1.
- 0.2: That's the chance it is late.

The complement rule: P(not A) = 1 − P(A). It's often the fastest route to an answer.
