# The mean is a balance point

> The mean adds every value and divides by how many there are. Think of it as the balance point: put the values on a seesaw and the mean is where it balances.

Lesson 1 of 5 in [Where is the middle](https://monte.markets/learn/center.md).

**Question:** Four friends have $2, $4, $6 and $8. If they pooled it and split it evenly, how much would each get?

- $4: Splitting evenly means total ÷ people: $20 ÷ 4.
- $6: Close, but $6 each would need $24 in the pot.
- **$5** (right answer): Yes. $20 shared four ways is $5. That's the mean.

The mean adds every value and divides by how many there are. Think of it as the balance point: put the values on a seesaw and the mean is where it balances.

**Question:** What is the mean of 3, 5, 7 and 25?

- **10** (right answer): Yes. 40 ÷ 4 = 10. Notice it sits above three of the four values, pulled by the 25.
- 6: That's the middle of 5 and 7, the median. The mean uses the totals.
- 7: Add them all: 3 + 5 + 7 + 25 = 40, then divide by 4.

Every value pulls on the mean, so a single big one can drag it a long way.
