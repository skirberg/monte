# The most common value

> The mode is the most common value. It's the only center that works for labels, like the most popular color or flavor.

Canonical URL: https://monte.markets/learn/center/mode/

Lesson 5 of 5 in [Where is the middle](https://monte.markets/learn/center.md).

**Question:** Shoe sizes sold today: 8, 9, 9, 10, 9, 11, 8. Which size should the shop restock first?

- 9, because it's the median: It is the median too, but the reason to restock is that it sells most often: the mode.
- **9** (right answer): Yes. It sold three times, more than any other. That's the mode.
- About 9.1, the mean: No one wears size 9.14. For restocking you want the size people actually buy most.

The mode is the most common value. It's the only center that works for labels, like the most popular color or flavor.

**Question:** Favorite ice cream in a class: vanilla 8, chocolate 12, strawberry 5. What is the center you can use?

- The median, vanilla: You can't sort flavors from small to big, so there's no middle one.
- **The mode, chocolate** (right answer): Yes. Flavors are labels, so there's no mean or median. The mode still works.
- The mean, 8.3: That's the average number of votes per flavor, not a typical flavor.

Mean for balance, median for typical, mode for most common. Pick the one that answers your question.
