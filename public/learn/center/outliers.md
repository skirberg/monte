# One extreme value

> The median is resistant: extreme values barely move it. The mean is not. That's why house prices and incomes are usually reported as medians.

**Question:** Nine people at a bar earn about $50k each. A billionaire walks in. What happens to the mean and the median?

- Neither changes: The mean adds every value, so a billion lands on it hard.
- **The mean soars, the median barely moves** (right answer): Yes. One huge value drags the mean. The middle person is still a $50k earner.
- Both soar: The median only cares about the middle position, and that's still a $50k person.

The median is resistant: extreme values barely move it. The mean is not. That's why house prices and incomes are usually reported as medians.

**Question:** A report says the average salary at a startup is $210k. The founder takes $2 million and everyone else about $90k. What should you ask for?

- A bigger sample: Every salary is already in. The issue is which summary to use.
- **The median** (right answer): Yes. The median shows what a typical person earns. The mean is pulled up by one salary.
- The total payroll: The total hides how it's split. You want the typical person.

When a few values are far from the rest, trust the median for typical.
