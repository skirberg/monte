# 68, 95, 99.7

> For normal data, about 68% fall within 1 SD of the mean, 95% within 2 SDs, and 99.7% within 3 SDs. Split the leftover evenly between the two tails.

**Question:** IQ scores are normal with mean 100 and SD 15. About what share score between 85 and 115?

- **About 68%** (right answer): Yes. That's within 1 SD of the mean.
- About 95%: 95% is within 2 SDs: 70 to 130.
- About 50%: Within 1 SD on both sides holds about two thirds.

For normal data, about 68% fall within 1 SD of the mean, 95% within 2 SDs, and 99.7% within 3 SDs.

**Question:** Same IQ scores. About what share score above 130?

- About 5%: That's both tails together. Above 130 is just one side.
- About 0.3%: That's outside 3 SDs (above 145 or below 55).
- **About 2.5%** (right answer): Yes. 130 is 2 SDs up. 5% fall outside 2 SDs, half on each side.

Split the leftover evenly between the two tails.
