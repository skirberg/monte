# Above, below and between

> Below z: Φ(z). Above z: 1 − Φ(z). Between a and b: Φ(b) − Φ(a). Below a negative z: Φ(−z) = 1 − Φ(z), by symmetry.

**Question:** For a normal curve, Φ(1) ≈ 0.84. What share lies above z = 1?

- About 84%: That's below.
- About 34%: That's between 0 and 1.
- **About 16%** (right answer): Yes. 1 − 0.84 = 0.16.

Below z: Φ(z). Above z: 1 − Φ(z). Between a and b: Φ(b) − Φ(a). Below a negative z: Φ(−z) = 1 − Φ(z), by symmetry.

**Question:** What share lies between z = −1 and z = 1?

- About 84%: That's everything below 1. Take off the part below −1.
- About 32%: That's the share outside, both tails.
- **About 68%** (right answer): Yes. Φ(1) − Φ(−1) = 0.84 − 0.16 = 0.68.
