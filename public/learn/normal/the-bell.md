# The bell shape

> The normal distribution is the bell curve. It's set by two numbers: the mean (where the peak is) and the SD (how wide it is).

**Question:** Heights of adult women: most are near the average, fewer are very short or very tall. What shape is that?

- **A bell** (right answer): Yes. Symmetric, peaked in the middle, thinning out on both sides.
- Flat: Flat would mean every height equally common.
- A long tail on one side: Heights are close to symmetric.

The normal distribution is the bell curve. It's set by two numbers: the mean (where the peak is) and the SD (how wide it is). Many measurements, and averages of many things, come out close to normal.

**Question:** Two normal curves share a mean. One has SD 5, the other SD 15. Which is wider and flatter?

- Same shape: The SD sets the width.
- **SD 15** (right answer): Yes. A bigger SD spreads the bell out.
- SD 5: A small SD makes a tall, narrow bell.

Mean slides the bell left and right. SD stretches it.
