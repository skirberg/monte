# From z to a probability

> To get a normal probability, convert to z, then look up Φ(z), the share of the curve below z. Φ(0) = 0.5, Φ(1) ≈ 0.84, Φ(2) ≈ 0.977. Φ gives the share below.

**Question:** Battery life is normal, mean 500 hours, SD 50. What z-score is 600 hours?

- **2** (right answer): Yes. (600 − 500) ÷ 50 = 2.
- 100: That's the gap in hours. Divide by the SD.
- 0.2: Divide the gap, 100, by the SD, 50.

To get a normal probability, convert to z, then look up Φ(z), the share of the curve below z. Φ(0) = 0.5, Φ(1) ≈ 0.84, Φ(2) ≈ 0.977.

**Question:** Same batteries. What share last less than 600 hours?

- About 2.3%: That's the share lasting more than 600.
- About 95%: 95% is between z = −2 and 2. Below 2 also includes the lower tail.
- **About 97.7%** (right answer): Yes. z = 2, and Φ(2) ≈ 0.977.

Φ gives the share below. For above, use 1 − Φ(z).
