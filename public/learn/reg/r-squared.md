# R²: how much the line explains

> R² is the share of the variation in y that the line explains, from 0 to 1. In simple regression, R² = r², the correlation squared. R² = r² in simple regression.

**Question:** A regression of sales on ad spend has R² = 0.64. What does that mean?

- **64% of the variation in sales is explained by ad spend** (right answer): Yes. The rest is left over in the residuals.
- Sales are 64% of ad spend: R² is about variation explained, not a ratio of amounts.
- The model is right 64% of the time: R² isn't a hit rate.

R² is the share of the variation in y that the line explains, from 0 to 1. In simple regression, R² = r², the correlation squared.

**Question:** The correlation between x and y is −0.8. What's R²?

- **0.64** (right answer): Yes. (−0.8)² = 0.64. The sign disappears when you square.
- −0.64: R² can't be negative in simple regression.
- 0.8: Square the correlation.

R² = r² in simple regression. High R² means a tight fit, not that x causes y.
