MONTE BRAND KIT
https://monte.markets/brand/   version 2026-11-23.09a7ea9

Monte is a free site for learning statistics by doing: lessons that start with a question, drills with fresh numbers, solvers that show every step, and one real chart a day.

WHAT IS IN EACH FOLDER
  logo/    The lockup, the clay curve beside "monte". colour (on light), reversed (on dark), black and white
           (one colour). SVG is the vector master; PNGs are transparent, 512, 1024 and 2048 px wide.
  mark/    The curve alone: colour, black, white, and tile (paper on clay, the app icon).
           SVG plus transparent PNGs at 64 to 1024 px.
  app/     The icons the site serves, byte for byte: favicon.svg and .ico, the Apple touch icon, the
           192, 512, maskable 512 and 1024 px icons.
  social/  Avatars, the link preview card and banners for X, LinkedIn, YouTube, GitHub, Instagram and
           stories, each sized for its platform and its crop. Wide banners also come in -dark.
  email/   The lockup 400 px wide for a 200 px signature, on white and transparent.
  colors/  The tokens as JSON and CSS, and colors.md: HEX, RGB, OKLCH, a CMYK approximation, and the
           contrast of every pair the site uses. monte.tokens.json is the source the site is built from
           (W3C Design Tokens format): the colours by mode, high contrast included, the corners and the motion.

USING THE LOGO
  Clear space: One x-height of the word on every side.
  Smallest size: the lockup 18 px tall on screen (6 mm in print); the mark 16 px.
  Monte, with a capital M, in a sentence.
  monte, lowercase, only inside the logo.
  monte.markets is the address, not the name.
  Never outline, stretch or recolour the curve, add a dot or sun above it, or set the word in another face.
  Clay means the number. Ink means the action.

VOICE
  A coach at practice. Short, plain, direct, a little dry.
  Do it, then say it. Monte says: "Heights of adult women: most are near the average, fewer are very short or very tall. What shape is that?" Never: "In this lesson you will learn about the normal distribution."
  One idea per sentence. Monte says: "Start the first idea, 4 min" Never: "Click here to begin your learning journey!"
  Show the work. Monte says: "Type in the numbers from any problem and get every answer with the work." Never: "Our powerful calculator does the hard part for you."
  Dry, never cute. Monte says: "Not quite. Here is the work." Never: "Oops! So close, you've got this!"
  For everyone. Monte says: "Is this test result real?" Never: "Ace your stats final with our Bayes calculator."
